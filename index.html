01 / Overview

What this initiative is building

Early-stage architectural decisions—massing, orientation, glazing, and envelope assemblies—shape building energy demand long before detailed engineering begins. Yet moving cleanly between Building Information Modelling (BIM) and physics-based simulation remains slow and fragmented.

Led by Principal Investigator Hadjer Bessai, Built Environment AI Initiative develops open protocols, transparent surrogate models, and lean computational workflows that keep human architectural judgment at the center.

01

Interpretable Modeling

Pairing energy simulation workflows with explainable machine learning (such as SHAP) rather than opaque black-box guesses.

02

Resource-Frugal Engineering

Practical, reproducible methods designed to run cleanly without requiring massive compute clusters.

03

Open Research Artifacts

Public protocols, clear governance, and documented datasets shared openly for researchers and practitioners.
