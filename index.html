Sheet 00 — Preface

Intelligible models for human-authored architecture.

The Built Environment AI Initiative is an open-science research effort at the intersection of architecture, Building Information Modelling, building performance simulation and applied machine learning. We build models designers can read, question and overrule — with modest compute, in the open.

The full website is still on the drawing board. Until it is issued, this sheet points to what is already public and what is being prepared.

Sheet 01 — Intent

What we're building

A body of open research, tools and documentation for early-stage design decisions, held to three working principles.

01

Interpretable by design

Models whose reasoning can be inspected. Feature attribution methods such as SHAP help architects see which design parameters drive a performance outcome, and why.

Explainable ML
02

Frugal in compute

Workflows that run on a laptop and a modest simulation budget. We favour small, well-framed experiments over hyperscale training and opaque pipelines.

BIM · Simulation
03

Authorship stays human

Machine learning is a drafting instrument, not an author. The architect frames the question, reads the evidence and makes the decision.
