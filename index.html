Core Research Principles

Interpretable Surrogates

Coupling BIM parameters and dynamic EnergyPlus simulations with explainable ML (SHAP, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty bounds).

Resource-Frugal AI

Designing lean tabular and geometric workflows accessible without hyperscale computing infrastructure.

Human-Centered Agency

Automating technical interoperability bottlenecks while preserving architectural authorship and engineering judgment.
